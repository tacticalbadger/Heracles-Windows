- Heracles now updates itself. It looks for a new version once a day and from **Help ▸ Check for Updates…**, shows what changed, and installs it when you agree. Installing restarts the app, after your notes are saved. **Help ▸ Check for Updates Automatically** turns the daily look off.
- This is the last version to put in by hand: later ones arrive in the app. Keep the Heracles folder in a place of your own on your PC, such as `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs`. Run from the disc, or from somewhere Windows keeps you from changing, it cannot replace itself, and says so.
- An update is installed only if it carries the developer's signature. A check sends the app's version and your PC's processor type, and nothing about your notes.
- Heracles has an icon: a notebook page with a heading, a checklist and lines of text. It is on the program, its windows, the taskbar and its reminders.
- The copy that an import makes of a library from the original Heracles is now named `heracles-original-<time>.sqlite`. Copies made earlier keep their names.
- Fixed: on a busy PC, a second copy of Heracles started just after the first could give up before the first was ready to take over from it.
